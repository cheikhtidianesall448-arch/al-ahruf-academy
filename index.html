Who we are

A welcoming place to learn, grow and flourish.

Al-Ahruf International Academy is an online Islamic educational institution committed to making Qur’anic and Islamic learning accessible to learners. We believe that education is a journey built on sincerity, consistency, guidance and care.

Our aim is to support each learner with structured lessons, dedicated teaching and a learning experience that respects their individual pace and goals.

What you can learn

Programmes for every step of your journey

Build your skills and deepen your understanding through guided learning and consistent practice.

ق

Qur’an Reading

Develop confidence, fluency and accuracy in reading the Holy Qur’an with guided lessons and regular practice.

ت

Tajwid

Learn the principles of correct recitation, articulation points and the rules that beautify Qur’anic reading.

ح

Hifz · Memorisation

Follow a structured memorisation journey supported by repetition, teacher guidance and achievable goals.

م

Qur’an Revision

Strengthen what you have memorised through regular revision, recitation and progress follow-up.

ف

Fiqh & Islamic Studies

Explore essential Islamic knowledge and develop a clearer understanding of faith and practice.

ع

Arabic Language

Develop your Arabic language skills as a foundation for deeper engagement with Islamic learning.
